Key Capabilities

Velocity-Based Forecasting

Predicts shortages by watching how fast stock is falling — not just how much is left. Monte Carlo simulation gives P10/P50/P90 stockout dates.

Actionable Early Alerts

CRITICAL / HIGH / WATCH tiers fire with enough lead time to act within government monthly indent cycles.

Inter-Facility Transfer Network

Automatically identifies nearby donor facilities with verified surplus and recommends safe, distance-ranked transfers.

Six-Role Access Control

Patients, pharmacists, CHC staff, district, state, and national officers each see only what they need — no more, no less.

Decision Support Only

StockWatch PHC is a clinical decision-support system only. All medicine transfers, emergency indents, and stock adjustments require explicit approval from an authorized Medical Officer. Forecasts are estimates and must never be acted on without human review.
